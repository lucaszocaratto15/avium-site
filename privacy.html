Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

The Avium is a management app for bird and poultry breeders. This policy describes what data we collect, why we collect it and how you can exercise your rights.

1. Data we collect

  • Account: name and email, for identification and sign-in.
  • App content: your aviary data (birds, flock, egg batches and hatches, supplies, finances, tasks, reminders) and photos you upload.
  • Team: if you invite staff, we store their link to your aviary.
  • Preferences: your app language, so notifications are sent in the right language.
  • Subscription: the status of your Premium subscription, reported by the App Store or Google Play through RevenueCat. We never receive your card details.

2. How we use it

  • To run the app: managing your flock, aviary and team.
  • To sync your data across devices and members of your aviary.
  • To control access to the Premium subscription.

3. Who we share it with

We do not sell your data. We do not show third-party ads. Data is stored on Supabase infrastructure (database and file storage), used as a technical provider, with encrypted traffic (HTTPS). Subscriptions are processed by the official stores (App Store / Google Play) through RevenueCat.

4. Privacy of your management data

Your aviary's internal data (birds, inventory, finances) is private: only members of your aviary can access it, enforced by access rules in the database (row-level security).

5. Your rights and account deletion

You can request access to or correction of your data at any time by emailing us at the address below. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA or Brazil's LGPD.

Full deletion of your account can be done directly in the app, in Settings → Delete my account. Deletion is permanent and removes your account, aviaries, birds, photos and all other data. Deleting your account does not cancel the store subscription; cancel it in your Apple ID settings or in Google Play. You can also request deletion by email.

6. Children

The Avium is not directed to children under 13.
